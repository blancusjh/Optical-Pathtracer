lroc_albedo_018.png is a material-albedo derivative, not a rendered-image edit.
Decode sRGB; multiply every linear RGB channel by 0.18/0.48217439031845044;
encode sRGB. Latitude-area-weighted mean diffuse reflectance = 0.18.
A uniform Lambert sphere then has geometric albedo 2/3 * 0.18 = 0.12,
approximately the Moon's geometric albedo; this is a Lambertian approximation,
not a calibrated lunar BRDF (no opposition surge, libration or displacement).
