THE GREGORIAN: mirror prescriptions — proposed format, not an engine patch

The scene is scenes/the_gregorian.owe. Existing material, mesh, world, assembly,
observer and placement syntax is unchanged. The six-row file is one Gregorian
optical prescription; previews/gregorian.py translates it into current bodies.
The current engine cannot load the requested original scene yet.

ROW GRAMMAR
label R t medium semi-diameter [k=value] [A4=value ...] [stop]
      [reflect material=Name substrate=length hole=diameter]
      [support=spider(count=N,width=length,angle=degrees,outer_radius=length)]
      [solve] [out_axis=(x,y,z)]

All lengths use the lens-file units. Units/wavelength/object_distance retain
existing meanings. R=inf is planar. semi-diameter is a radius, ALWAYS; hole is a
full DIAMETER. medium is the physical medium after the encounter; reflect does
not change it, and must not manufacture negative-index physical glass.

reflect: optional bare flag. Without it the row retains existing refraction or
iris behavior. On a reflecting row k and the even asphere coefficients retain
their existing sag definition. material resolves an opaque Mirror or Conductor
material in the containing scene (mandatory for reflect; do not silently choose
aluminium). substrate is the mirror's physical axial backing depth (mandatory,
positive). The front uses that material; back, edge and hole bore absorb.
The backing lies on the nonilluminated side of the reflecting face, clears the maximum sag,
and never fills the central bore. hole defaults to 0, and must be <2*sd.
stop on the primary marks the entrance aperture, not a second overlapping iris.
The stop's image is an annulus; a geometrically blocked centre is never an
emitter and is not replaced with an intensity mask.

support defaults to absent. It makes actual opaque supports behind the mirror's
back, shadowing the incoming beam; no diffraction or image-space mask. count is
an integer >=1, width is its tangential width, angle the first support's azimuth
about local +z from +x; supports are equally spaced. outer_radius is the support
endpoint in that row's mechanical frame. Its inside attachment meets the back
of the secondary; finite axial thickness equals substrate. In this scan one
1.2mm strut reaches the bore radius 54mm; its contact shoe is included in the
same solid and must meet the measured tube, not penetrate it. With tube=true,
a mounting shoe reaches the generated tube wall. Omission means no magically
floating secondary: the containing assembly must supply an existing mount.
A concave secondary's opaque backing already creates its circular obstruction;
its diameter is 2*sd. No additional coincident black disc is built.

solve: optional bare row flag marking the ONE air gap t adjusted by afocal=true.
This is part of folded prescription spacing, not a new scene keyword. It is
useful because the longest physical segment need not be the afocal adjustment.
Require exactly one marked nonterminal gap in a mirror prescription with an
eyepiece; its magnitude must remain positive along the outgoing ray. Solve C=0
at the design wavelength while retaining mirror separation and all glass
thicknesses. Never solve by moving the primary-secondary pair or hiding blur.
The Gregorian secondary-to-field-lens gap is marked; the primary-secondary gap
is fixed. A signed segment is propagated with its actual sign throughout.

SIGN CONVENTION (coaxial case)
At the first surface set a fixed mechanical +z parallel to the incoming light.
R is the signed radius in this fixed chart: z(r)=r^2/(2R)+... . It does NOT flip
because the light reflected. t is the signed displacement to the NEXT vertex,
NOT an always-positive unfolded path length. Surface order is encounter order,
not sorted spatial order. After the primary the ray travels -z and t is negative;
after the secondary it travels +z and t is positive. Glass thickness still
follows its actual geometric orientation, grouped only on transmitted rows.
Mirror substrates, hole walls and traversed bore planes are NOT optical rows.

For ray state (height y, geometric slope u=dy/dz), use
 P(t)=[[1,t],[0,1]]
 mirror(R)=[[1,0],[-2/R,-1]]
 refract(R,n1,n2)=[[1,0],[-(n2-n1)/(n2*R),n1/n2]]
with physical positive indices. After a reflection the outgoing direction and
signed segment agree. This convention gives P(-f)*mirror(-2f) a focus for
parallel rays, and does not mistake a Gregorian for a Cassegrain.

out_axis: optional on reflect only; for a noncoaxial fold, the outgoing chart's
+z is reset to this unit direction in the containing prescription's original
mechanical frame. The flat mirror normal bisects the incident and outgoing
physical directions (normalize(d_in-d_out)); a right-handed transverse basis
is parallel-transported, never reset to an arbitrary camera up. The ensuing
t is positive along the new chart. Default: keep the chart unchanged, so a
coaxial reflection reverses travel and changes the sign of t. A flat fold has
zero power; frame changes transport transverse orientation explicitly. Reject
out_axis on a powered mirror unless the engine implements its decentered,
tilted optical mapping; no pretending it is coaxial.

AFocal, PUPIL AND TUBE
Afocal optics register the actual exit-pupil conjugate after all encounters,
including the reflecting stop surface. exit_pupil("Gregorian") returns that
world position using the final outgoing frame. Signed angular magnification
is positive for the Gregorian, negative for a Newtonian in transported image
coordinates. Report effective objective focal length, eyepiece EFL, D/|M| and
pupil conjugates; C approximately zero is the afocal check. Pupil guiding must
use the annular pupil and support-vane exclusions with correct density; retain
a full-pupil mixture for ghosts and stray light. It must not divide away the
~40% speculum loss or the blocked area. Existing observers are unchanged.

tube=true builds a physical black-inside, tube_material-outside shell over the
SPATIAL envelope of the actual folded ray path, not the algebraic sum of t and
not a giant unfolded cylinder. Clearances account for signed sag and backing.
It also builds nonintersecting primary/secondary mounting shoes, opaque bore
baffles and a narrow rear ocular barrel. Primary and rear mounts retain their
central holes. After a Newtonian fold it includes the side focuser and a real
opening at the fold. Never put an end cap across the primary bore or secondary
path; check all rays over the stated field. A casing never duplicates a scanned
housing. Here tube=false deliberately uses the existing scanned shell instead.
For tube_radius, use it as a minimum clear main-bore radius; reject insufficient
clearance rather than intersecting a mirror rim. The stand is never generated.

Gregorian's powered concave secondary, it does not reimage the intermediate
focus. Newtonian angular image parity remains inverted; do not equate counting
mirror reflections with counting image inversions. Its exit pupil diameter is
100/12.73943=7.84965mm. Transverse basis transport accounts for the fold's
reflection parity, rather than rotating the image by camera-up convention.

Speculum: polished copper-tin empirical mirror spectrum rgb(0.65,0.625,0.60),
a slightly warm reflectance approximation, no aluminium or invented copper
optical constants. At 587.5618nm the engine spectral reconstruction gives
R=0.6357615633: two reflections transmit 0.4041927653; four crown-air
interfaces leave 0.3403 before geometric obstruction/pupil averaging.
Brass is metal=gold with the scan's UV photo and roughness 0.30 for wear.
The empirical conductor tint is texture-dependent; the photograph provides
its actual spatial variation. Dark woods range 0.039..0.14 linear reflectance.

Moon: NASA CGI Moon Kit LROC map, north up, sphere radius 1737400m, range
384400000m. Its angular diameter is 0.5179deg. It is a diffuse mesh lit by
a 5778K Sun at azimuth 245deg/elevation -20deg, with an Earth sphere screening
the below-horizon source from the room. Albedo is physically normalized in
LINEAR material space to mean diffuse reflectance 0.18, giving a Lambert-sphere
geometric-albedo approximation near 0.12; no image-space painted light.

VERIFICATION
Paraxial vs emit real rays at 587.5618nm:
  Effective two-mirror focal length: 2115.000 vs 2114.811mm.
  Magnification: +53.887780 vs +53.941761 (differential ray fan at r=24mm).
  Exit diameter: 2.004165 vs 2.000392mm (outer marginal-ray envelope).
  Exit z from primary: 137.828722 vs 137.820052mm.
  Exit distance after last lens: 14.740078 vs 14.731409mm.
  Two-mirror back focus: 105.000000 vs 105.000000mm.
Residual finite-aperture differences reflect aberration and finite-ray height,
not a hidden eye shift. The scratch eye stays at the computed pupil.

Obstruction: 42/108=0.388888889. emit inner/outer radii 0.389785/1.000196mm
give 0.389708294. GPU light sensor, 2mm beyond the pupil conjugate: fitted
inner/outer radii 0.388749/0.999193mm give 0.389063604 (15um radial bins,
about 0.015 ratio tolerance). Support-vane sector remains visible and is
excluded from the ideal circular-envelope formula; it reduces intensity
locally, not the outer pupil diameter.
An afocal eyepiece has no finite point focus. The requested behind-eyepiece
annulus is therefore a screen slightly defocused from the PUPIL conjugate.
The point-source test uses a Sun of angular diameter 0.0001deg (point at
infinity approximation), solely on an isolated optical bench, traced with
light. It is not present as a light in the artistic scene.

Erect Moon: same illuminated-left terminator, lunar north and maria arrangement
in Eyepiece and the fov-matched Naked diagnostic. Central-disk smoothed linear
correlation 0.95762 erect vs -0.78887 after 180deg rotation. Remove the broad
phase gradient: lunar-feature correlation 0.84208 erect vs 0.14966 after
rotation; vertical flip -0.05740, horizontal flip 0.17989. Positive measured
angular magnification independently confirms erect orientation. These are
NUMERICAL comparisons of raw images; final PNGs receive no denoising, painted
optical effects or image manipulation. Statistical limitations remain at 32
iterations: the eyepiece preview has visible spectral Monte Carlo grain.

CURRENT ENGINE PROBLEMS (not fixed)
1. Mirror rows are unavailable, intentionally the new specification:
 build/owe info scenes/the_gregorian.owe --backend gpu
 -> unexpected token 'reflect' at lenses/gregorian_54x.lens:11.
2. OBJ UVs are discarded, so texture=mapping=uv rejects textured OBJ files:
 build/owe info out/scenes/gregorian/obj_uv_repro.owe --backend gpu
 Workaround: scientific lunar sphere exported as glTF, UVs retained. No parser fix.
3. emit accepts --backend gpu but cmdEmit still uses host double Tracer:
 build/owe emit out/scenes/gregorian/emit.owe --backend gpu --from 0,0.04,-0.7 --dir 0,0,1 --primary --cone 0 --rays 1
 The requested emit numbers are diagnostic host results, not GPU transport
results; independent image orientation and annulus measurements ARE GPU.
4. GPU region inconsistencies/leaks persist around opaque contacts and distant
Earth in short VCM renders despite disjoint scene bodies:
 build/owe render out/scenes/gregorian/preview.owe --backend gpu --device 1 --detector Beside --integrator vcm --spp 32 --resolution 320x200 --out out/scenes/gregorian/repro_regions
 Logs contain exact boundary/ray events; do not claim all are proven engine
defects rather than mixed-winding scan contact sensitivity. No fixes made.
5. Direct narrow-field distant Moon is almost black with GPU path while VCM
resolves it at the same geometry, exposure and sampling:
 build/owe render out/scenes/gregorian/preview.owe --backend gpu --device 1 --detector Naked --integrator path --spp 32 --resolution 320x200 --set Naked.fov=0.732169deg --out out/scenes/gregorian/naked_detail_path
 Compare with integrator=vcm: mean Y about 8.77e-8 vs 0.0055722. Mechanism
unresolved; preserved as a repro, not an asserted diagnosis.
6. A VCM cold-cache compilation stalled and hit the 119s timeout; the next
Eyepiece/Naked renders completed in seconds. Concurrent work also compiled
VCM kernels on this host. Repro with an already cold driver cache:
 python previews/gregorian.py --views Beside --spp 32
This is a build/driver/cache observation, not a
claim that this scene's GPU transport takes 119s.
