## Filament clearance

Each of the six lead-in wires (0.05 mm radius) stops 30 µm short of its carbon loop (2 mm torus, 0.08 mm tube). The gap is measured from the wire's whole flat end disc, which meets the loop obliquely, to both the analytic tube and the 48 × 12 mesh the loader builds. When the wires ended on the loops' centre circles, part of each emitting surface lay inside an opaque wire, so light started inside another body and VCM counted region inconsistencies on about 0.6 % of paths; gallery promotion requires none. Lead2A and Lead3B cross their loops as chords, so they stop before the first crossing instead of on the far side. The 0.8 % of loop area that was buried now radiates, so each loop emits its full 41.469 lm and the views render about 1 % brighter. At gallery resolution a pixel spans at least 0.5 mm at the lamp, so the gap cannot be seen, and the loops' size, position and emission are unchanged.
