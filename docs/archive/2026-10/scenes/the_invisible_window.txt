10 OCTOBER 2026 — COMPLETED ROOM AND WALL GALLERY

The adopted scene uses mapped lime plaster, fumed oak and marble, with Room and
Wall framing that includes the complete apparatus/projection. Nineteen physical
optical blocks, including Sun, PMMA panel, heliostat, receiver, shutters and
frames, are byte-identical across this material/camera adoption. The frozen panel

A corrected-engine estimator review compared native Room VCM 16, SPPM 16 and VCM 192
images. SPPM retained clearer plaster, floor and ceiling structure at comparable
cost, so the final budgets are Room 64 and Wall 128, each with 16 photons per pixel,
depth 10, seed 7, one pass at 960×600. This visual choice is not an equal-error or
convergence measurement. Raw images receive no denoising or retouching.

Room 64 is reviewed and promoted to docs/gallery/invisible_window_room.png.
The complete transparent panel and projected lancet have clear separation;
plaster, timber, ceiling junction and tile joints remain legible. Fine grain and
coloured points around the pane/dark receiver remain. The near-white projection
retains face, hands and banderole linework at Room EV -12; Wall EV -15 is the detail
view. Native transport took 943.538 s (process 961.577 s), with 626,688,000 paths,
3,284,531,893 segments, 168 region inconsistencies and 21 leaks. Raw RGB is finite.
These diagnostics remain unresolved; local visual acceptance is not a claim of
clean transport.

Wall 128 is also reviewed and promoted to docs/gallery/invisible_window_wall.png.
The entire oblique lancet fits with clearance at the arch and bottom. Portrait,
raised hand, drapery and ribbon structure remain readable at EV -15; the ribbon's
lettering is not legible. Optical softness and sampling grain limit fine detail,
and sparse coloured points remain in the dark surround. Both root and independent
review accepted the native image for this bounded local gallery milestone.
Transport took 1782.620 s (process 1799.341 s), with 1,253,376,000 paths,
6,646,668,483 segments, 243 region inconsistencies and 34 leaks. Raw RGB is finite.
The first diagnostic is at Heliostat.face with Panel.interior[PMMA]. Neither
review nor zero postprocessing removes these unresolved transport limitations.

Both captures and their raw data/logs are retained under
